# How to Calculate Automation ROI for Client Proposals

## Overview
Return on Investment (ROI) is the ultimate metric that matters to clients. By calculating and presenting clear ROI figures, you can justify automation projects and win more deals.

**Key Formula:** ROI = (Gains - Costs) / Costs × 100%

---

## Part 1: Understanding ROI Components

### Direct Cost Savings
- **Labor Hours Saved** × Hourly Rate = Annual Savings
  - Example: 10 hours/week × 52 weeks × $50/hour = $26,000/year
- **Tools and Software Eliminated** = License Cost Savings
- **Error Reduction** = Rework Cost Avoided

### Indirect Benefits (Harder to Quantify)
- Improved accuracy (fewer customer complaints)
- Faster processing (better customer satisfaction)
- Employee morale (less repetitive work)
- Competitive advantage (faster time to market)

---

## Part 2: Calculating Labor Savings

### Step 1: Identify Current Process
- List all steps (e.g., invoice entry, approval, payment)
- Time each step (best, average, worst case)
- Count frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)

### Step 2: Estimate Automation Impact
- Which steps are eliminated? (100% savings)
- Which steps are accelerated? (50% savings)
- What's the total time saved per cycle?

### Step 3: Calculate Annual Impact
```
Time per cycle (hours) × Cycles per year × Hourly rate = Annual savings
```

**Example: Invoice Processing**
- Current: 15 minutes per invoice
- Automated: 1 minute per invoice
- Savings: 14 minutes per invoice
- Volume: 500 invoices/month × 12 = 6,000/year
- Annual savings: 6,000 × (14/60) × $40/hour = **$56,000/year**

---

## Part 3: Implementation Costs

### One-Time Costs
- Analysis and design: 20-40 hours
- Development: 40-200 hours (depends on complexity)
- Testing: 10-20 hours
- Training: 5-10 hours
- Total: 75-270 hours × $50-150/hour = $3,750-$40,500

### Recurring Costs (Annual)
- Tool subscriptions: $100-$500/month
- Maintenance: 2-4 hours/month
- Updates and improvements: 5-10% of implementation cost

---

## Part 4: ROI Calculation Examples

### Example 1: Invoice Processing Automation
- Annual labor savings: $56,000
- Implementation cost: $10,000
- Annual tool cost: $2,000
- **Year 1 ROI:** (56,000 - 10,000 - 2,000) / (10,000 + 2,000) × 100 = **440%**
- **Payback period:** (10,000 + 2,000) / 56,000 = 0.21 years = **2.5 months**

### Example 2: Lead Qualification Automation
- Annual labor savings: $35,000
- Implementation cost: $8,000
- Annual tool cost: $1,200
- **Year 1 ROI:** (35,000 - 8,000 - 1,200) / (8,000 + 1,200) × 100 = **315%**
- **Payback period:** (8,000 + 1,200) / 35,000 = 0.26 years = **3 months**

### Example 3: Data Entry Automation
- Annual labor savings: $24,000
- Implementation cost: $6,000
- Annual tool cost: $600
- **Year 1 ROI:** (24,000 - 6,000 - 600) / (6,000 + 600) × 100 = **289%**
- **Payback period:** (6,000 + 600) / 24,000 = 0.275 years = **3.3 months**

---

## Part 5: Presenting ROI to Clients

### Build a Simple Spreadsheet
```
Year 1
├─ Annual labor savings: $56,000
├─ Implementation cost: -$10,000
├─ Tool cost (annual): -$2,000
├─ Net benefit: $44,000
└─ ROI: 440%

Year 2 (no implementation cost)
├─ Annual labor savings: $56,000
├─ Tool cost (annual): -$2,000
└─ Net benefit: $54,000
└─ ROI: 2,700%
```

### Key Messages for Clients
1. **Payback period:** "You'll recover your investment in X months"
2. **Ongoing savings:** "Every year after, you save $X with no additional investment"
3. **Hidden benefits:** "Plus improved accuracy, faster processing, and happier employees"

### Avoid Common Mistakes
- ❌ Overstating savings (be conservative)
- ❌ Ignoring maintenance costs
- ❌ Forgetting implementation time
- ❌ Assuming 100% process improvement (usually 60-80%)

---

## Part 6: Sensitivity Analysis

### Best Case Scenario
- Savings 25% higher than estimate
- Costs 20% lower than estimate
- Example: $56,000 × 1.25 = $70,000 savings with $8,000 cost = 775% ROI

### Worst Case Scenario
- Savings 25% lower than estimate
- Costs 20% higher than estimate
- Example: $56,000 × 0.75 = $42,000 savings with $12,000 cost = 300% ROI

**Always present a realistic range, not just best-case numbers.**

---

## Summary

**Calculate ROI to win more deals:**
- Quantify labor savings in hours and dollars
- Include all implementation and ongoing costs
- Present clear payback periods and year-by-year benefits
- Show sensitivity analysis for different scenarios
- Use ROI as your strongest selling tool

**Key Takeaway:**
Most automation projects pay for themselves in 2-4 months. After that, it's pure profit.
