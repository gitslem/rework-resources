# 10 Business Processes Every Company Should Automate First

## Overview
This guide identifies the 10 most universally applicable, high-ROI processes that virtually every company can automate, regardless of industry or size. These are proven quick wins that generate immediate value.

---

## Process #1: Lead Nurturing & Email Sequences

**What It Is:**
Automatically send targeted emails to leads based on their actions or characteristics.

**Why Automate:**
- Manual sending: 5-10 min per lead
- Automated: 0 min (happens instantly)
- ROI: Converts more leads, saves time

**Implementation:**
```
New signup
  ↓
Email 1 (Day 1): Welcome
  ↓
Email 2 (Day 3): Value proposition
  ↓
Email 3 (Day 7): Case study
  ↓
Email 4 (Day 14): Call to action
```

**Time to Set Up:** 2-3 hours
**Time Saved Per Month:** 5-10 hours
**Best Platforms:** Zapier, Make
**Payoff:** Within days (leads convert faster)

## Process #2: Invoice & Receipt Processing

**What It Is:**
Automatically extract data from invoices and input into accounting system.

**Why Automate:**
- Manual data entry: 3-5 min per invoice
- Manual: 50 invoices = 2.5-4 hours/month
- Automated: 30 seconds per invoice (with AI)

**Implementation:**
```
Invoice received
  ↓
Extract data (amount, date, vendor, account)
  ↓
Validate (does amount match PO?)
  ↓
Route to approval (if >$1000, needs approval)
  ↓
Categorize & record
```

**Time to Set Up:** 4-6 hours
**Time Saved Per Month:** 10-20 hours
**Best Platforms:** Make, n8n (for AI extraction)
**Payoff:** First week

## Process #9: Follow-up & Reminder Sequences

**What It Is:**
Automatically send reminders and follow-ups based on conditions.

**Why Automate:**
- Manual follow-ups: Easily forgotten
- Automated: Never miss a deadline
- Improvement: 20-30% improvement in conversion from follow-ups

**Implementation:**
```
Sales email sent
  ↓
3 days: No response? Send reminder
  ↓
5 days: No response? Send second reminder
  ↓
7 days: No response? Move to cold list
  ↓
Schedule check-in in CRM
```

**Time to Set Up:** 1-2 hours
**Time Saved Per Month:** 4-6 hours (+ conversion gain)
**Best Platforms:** Zapier, Make
**Payoff:** Within week (improved follow-up rate)

## Process #10: Expense & Time Tracking

**What It Is:**
Automatically collect and categorize expenses and time entries.

**Why Automate:**
- Manual collection: People forget to submit
- Automated: Capture at source (receipt email, etc.)
- Speed: Same-day processing possible

**Implementation:**
```
Employee forwards receipt email
  ↓
Extract amount, vendor, date
  ↓
Categorize (R&D, Travel, Meals, etc.)
  ↓
Add to reimbursement report
  ↓
Auto-approve if under $50
  ↓
Route to manager if over $50
```

**Time to Set Up:** 2-3 hours
**Time Saved Per Month:** 3-5 hours
**Best Platforms:** Make, n8n
**Payoff:** Second week
